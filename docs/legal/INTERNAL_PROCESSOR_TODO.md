# Internal processor checklist

This file is internal operational documentation and is not customer-facing.

## External accounting

- [ ] Add the legal name and identification of the external accounting firm to the internal processor register.
- [ ] Verify a GDPR data-processing agreement or corresponding contractual clauses.
- [ ] Record categories of data, purpose, access method, retention and deletion process.
- [ ] Confirm whether any sub-processors or transfers outside the EEA are involved.

Public privacy wording remains the general category: "externí účetní, daňoví a případně právní poradci".

## E-mail and infrastructure

- [ ] Add Resend to the processor register and review its DPA and processing locations.
- [ ] Keep Ecomail listed only if the newsletter integration is activated.
- [ ] Confirm Cloudflare and Supabase production contracts, regions and subprocessors.

Final wording and retention periods require review by a Czech lawyer before launch.
